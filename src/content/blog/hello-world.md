I spent a career going under the covers of the software I use: finding out how
things actually work, where performance excels, where it suffers, and then
teaching what I learned to other people.

This blog is that habit, written down.

The posts here follow one rule: measured, not eyeballed. Claims come with
numbers, numbers come with method, and when I did not verify something, I say
so. Expect deep dives on frontend mechanics: how frameworks schedule work,
what change detection actually costs, why some architectures age well and
others accumulate debt in installments.

learning React.
